## Improvement Opportunities

1. **Enhance Service Levels**:
   - Implement the suggested safety stock and reorder point adjustments
   - Focus on improving lowest-performing stores first

2. **Reduce Stockout Risk**:
   - Prioritize high-risk products for inventory review
   - Increase buffer stock for top risk products

3. **Optimize Pricing**:
   - Apply recommended price increases/decreases to achieve 1% revenue improvement
   - Monitor price elasticity and customer response to adjustments

4. **Improve Forecasting**:
   - Enhance forecasting methods with more sophisticated models
   - Incorporate more external factors and seasonality into demand predictions

## Potential Business Impact

Based on our analysis, implementing the recommended improvements could result in:

- **Revenue increase**: At least 1% through optimized pricing
- **Service level improvement**: Potential to reach industry standard 95% service level

The overall performance assessment indicates several areas requiring immediate attention, with service levels and stockout risk management being the highest priorities.
